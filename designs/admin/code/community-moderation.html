Community Module
Turns the entire Community section on or off for all users. Existing spaces and posts are preserved while disabled.

Features

Discussions & Q&A COM-001
The general community feed where members post and reply.
Groups & Channels COM-002
Named spaces scoped to a topic, course, cohort, or team.
Announcements COM-003
Pinned, targeted broadcasts to the whole org, a group, or an org unit.
Moderation COM-004
Reporting, the moderation queue, and pin/remove/warn actions.

Community module is off

Discussions, Groups & Channels, Announcements, and Moderation are all hidden while the module is disabled. Turn it back on to configure them.
